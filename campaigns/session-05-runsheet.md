## 🎬 RUN OF SHOW

### 0 · DECK — the boarding action
**Screen:** DECK chip. The Crossing is now portrait — bow top, stern bottom, rails
down both long sides, 8 × 12 squares (40 × 60 ft).
**Combat:** HUD → load encounter → **The Crossing — boarding action** (4× Tide-Taken,
HP 22 · AC 12) → roll initiative → Board → **+ PARTY**, then **+ FOES (FROM COMBAT)**.
Standees resolve automatically.
**Mechanic to watch:** the fight pulls creatures TO the rails. The long deck means a
shove always has a rail near it — that's the shape working.
**Test:** damage a Tide-Taken from the tracker; the number floats on the board and the
players' table updates.

### 1 · THE STAIR — the descent
**Screen:** THE STAIR chip. Narration only. No tokens needed.

### 2 · FLOOD GATE
**Screen:** FLOOD GATE chip. **Board + narration only** — no explorable, no puzzle
screen. REV 3 deleted the letter mechanic entirely and the seeded puzzle board is gone.
Run the room from the table doc.

### 3 · NAVE — the covenant stone
**Screen:** NAVE chip, then **PANELS → 🪨 Nave: Covenant** when they approach the tablet.
**On the panel:** ASHMANTLE · DUNMORE · —ARWOOD and the oath. "Press a bloodied thumb
to a sigil" → the silver wakes and stays lit. Repeat use has its own line.
**DM beat:** Creed recognising his own mark is yours to give at the table. The page
never names houses beyond those three.

### 4 · CORRIDOR
**Screen:** CORRIDOR chip. **Board + narration only.** The lit path is painted ON the
map — they pick their steps by it. Step the glow = safe; a dark step is DC 12 DEX or
1d4 cold + grappled one round. Wall script is texture; nothing to read, nothing to click.

### 5 · BELL WELL — the choice
**Screen:** BELL WELL chip **only if they ring.** If they climb, stay off the screen.
**If rung:** HUD → load **Bell Well — the sleepers** (3× Drowned Reacher, HP 16 · AC 12)
→ initiative → board. They wear the Tide-Taken standee by design.
**Quiet cost:** clear the sleepers here and her bell lair action is spent. Say nothing.

### 6 · CELL — the soul
**Screen:** NOTHING. Dim it. Tech would wound this room. Never cut it.

### 7 · DOOR — the Held Door
**Screen:** DOOR chip (mood only), then **PANELS → 🚪 The Door / 🚪 Four Stations**.
**The art:** four stone hands lying open, palms up. Three carry a worn house mark;
**the third from the left is hacked out.**
**🕳 Hidden hotspot:** click the defaced palm **only when a player points at it.** No
pin, no cursor change — it is spotted or it isn't.
**Then:** she opens the door herself. All four crowns die at once.

### 8 · HEART — the boss
**Screen:** HEART chip for position; **PANELS → 🕯 Heart: Lantern** before dice.
**🕳 Hidden hotspot:** Edrik is behind the joint in the wall **right of the lantern
dais**. Perception DC 13, or stand quiet and hear breathing. Click on discovery only.
**The spring — first trigger wins:** they find him or demand him · they refuse or stall ·
Willa's hands touch the lantern and his pounding interrupts.
**Then:** HUD → load **The Heart — Nerea** (HP 114 · AC 14) → initiative → board →
press **`B`** for boss mode and don't leave the board again.

## ☠️ BOSS MODE — what the tracker does for you

Press `B` in the Companion.

- **HP ticker** ±1/5/10. Phase banners light themselves off damage dealt: **40 →**
  Multiattack · **75 →** +Whelm. The **1-HP floor** holds her up until you tick
  "P3 has taken a turn."
- **Lair actions** (init 20, pick one) strike through when used and clear on
  **next round ▶**.
- **Edrik freed** reveals his line *and* removes Chains Tighten from the list.
- **Mira boarded** adds her card with its own HP.
- **NEVER damage-Whelm Willa** is printed on the panel where you'll see it.

**Reachers/Tide-Taken tactics:** open on Claw. Switch a damaged or ignored one to
Icy Bite. Never all four on Bite.
