# SESSION 4 — CHAPEL EXPLORABLE: DM NOTES 🗝

Companion to `session-04-chapel.html` (artifact "The Chapel Above Blackreef").
Built to the Claude-chat handoff spec 2026-07-25 — where that spec and older
notes disagree, the spec won. The page is player-safe: no keys, no answers,
and the ward-word appears nowhere in its code. Let them view source.

## How each clickable runs

- **THE ARCHIVE** — three ledger texture cards + a *"search the rolls by
  family name"* box. Player-typed names hit canned entries: **Thornwood**
  (the Mooring, two daughters), **Thorne, Edrik** ("pays in other ways"),
  **Pike** ("See also: Pike"), and the big one — **Ashmantle**: the house
  lamp, silver and wickless, withdrawn from the order's keeping.
  🗝 Don't prompt it. Let Cory think to search his own name — or not.
  Anything else: "No entry in the rolls. The sea keeps its own census."
- **THE BLESSING OF THE KEPT LIGHT** (added 07-26; open book on the reading
  stand in the Archive panel) — the rosetta. Glyph line over "THE HEARTH IS
  HOME." Shared shapes with the refrain: ⬟=H ✦=E ●=O ▲=M ✚=I — ⬟✦ they
  already know; ●▲✚ are the new cracks, after which the refrain reads
  C O M E H O M E C H I L D minus only C/L/D — effectively solved. T▼ R⬢
  A◐ S✖ appear nowhere in the refrain (deliberate noise; note the spec's
  claim that I is unused was wrong — I is in the refrain, so it shares ✚).
  🗝 Say NOTHING when they open it. The matching IS the puzzle; the young
  hand's marginal note is the only nudge. Expect the refrain to fall fast
  once anyone starts comparing shapes — budget the kindle moment for it.
- **MAREN'S TABLE** — the thirteen marks, static, with only the two margin
  notes (= E, = H). "Open the living transcription →" opens the live glyph
  board; you drive the solve from 🧩 Puzzles as scripted.
- **THE WARDED PAGE** — a DIFFERENT page than Halve's: one that's kept Maren
  humble for nine years. The input has **no correct answer** — every word
  gets a decline. 🗝 Purpose: they learn what a keyed ward IS on a harmless
  example, so when Halve's page hits the table and a word actually WORKS,
  the moment lands. If they ask Maren for hints: she has none. Nine years.
- **THE ALTAR** — the kept light, leaning toward the sea (the flame overlay
  on the room image visibly leans — if someone notices, the answer is
  "…yes?"). "Kneel a moment" gives one warm line, same for everyone.
- **THE IRON RING** — its marker is nearly invisible and glints COLD, not
  candle-warm; rewards whoever sweeps the room slowly. "Touch it" gives the
  cold-iron line. 🗝 **No explanation exists anywhere. The vagueness is
  load-bearing. Do not improvise an answer tonight** — whatever it moors is
  future canon.

## Running it

Share the artifact from its share menu → drop the link in Discord at the
chapel scene (offline copy: `campaigns/session-04-chapel.html` opens
double-click, no internet). Visited candles dim so the table can see what's
been searched. Rolls stay at the table — the page never adjudicates anything.
