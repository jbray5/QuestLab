## 🧩 Running the two puzzles

**Before the chapel scene:** open 🧩 Puzzles, hit **▸ run it** on *The
Refrain*, and put its **player link** on the projector (or in Teams chat —
it works in both).

**The Refrain (glyph board)**
- The board opens showing exactly Maren's two sounds: **✦ = E, ⬟ = H**.
- As the table reasons, type letters into the glyph boxes in the workbench —
  every instance updates on the projector instantly.
- **🗣 Speak** logs a full reading. Wrong → the display *hums* and the hum
  counter ticks (that's your cue to narrate a consequence that night).
  Right → the board kindles and fills.
- **🖐 players CAN touch** lets them assign letters from their own phones —
  optional; the default is DM-only.
- Stuck? **💡 solve** fills the answer (the floor from the addendum's stuck
  ladder).

**Halve's Page (cipher)**
- Opens **warded** — letters visibly swim, unselectable. That's the whole
  "it doesn't want reading" beat, live.
- When someone says AELIM, type it into **🔑 Speak**. Correct → the page
  *stills*; read the LOCK 1 line.
- Hand-decoding: players tap a letter on the display and pick what it
  becomes; correct letters lock green. The alignment matches Maren's
  method exactly (A steps 0, E steps 4, L 11, I 8, M 12; spaces skipped).
- **👁 reveal word / line / all** when energy dips. **📖 read plaintext**
  gives you Appendix B to read aloud.
- When the first sentence resolves the workbench reminds you to narrate
  the thread going slack — **that's DM-only, never shown to players**.
