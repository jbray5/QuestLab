Welcome back to Hollowmere, everybody!

(wait — "Hollowmere!")

Last session: you leveled up, and the town of Silverford threw you a breakfast. The kids reenacted your battle. Then the wolves. Four wolves, running from something, and this party — this party of MERCY, sparers of flower girls and sprites — went four for four. No survivors. Thane personally accounted for THREE, crossbow and blade, earning the title he will never, ever shed: the Dog Murderer of Silverford.

(beat)

Then, at dusk — a crooked wagon that wasn't there a moment ago. The Tallyman. Lovely fella. GREAT prices. Nya bought a whistle that summons the Wild Hunt — cost her nothing but her true name, whispered once. Cory bought a dagger that knows every lie in the room — cost: the last kind lie he'll ever tell, whenever that turns out to be. Hayley bought a purse that never runs out of coin — for one true secret, still owed. And Parker — Parker bought a jar of BOTTLED NIGHTMARES, and paid with a night's sleep, to be collected… whenever the Tallyman feels like it. Everyone slept great. So far.

(slow down)

And then the fire burned low. And at the edge of the light — the stag. Bark through its hide. Eyes black, corner to corner. Like hers. It looked at each of you. And it walked into the dark, where no severed shrine should be.

You chose the west road. The coast. Blackreef Cove — where the tide has learned to sing.

(beat — grin)

But before we get on that road… somebody still owes the Tallyman.

## 3 ▸ THE ROAD WEST + THE FORD (:25)

### 🏮 THE LANTERN FORD — FULL WALKTHROUGH (dusk)

📖 "The road drops to a ford — and the ford is gone. Rain-swollen water, fast and cold, too deep to wade with gear. On the near bank: two rotted winch posts, a sunken flatboat, and a leaning post with an old ferry-lantern hanging from it. The lantern has no wick. At the post's foot sits a small stone basin — bone dry. A worn brass plate reads: 'LIGHT CARRIES. DARK KEEPS.'" 🗝 A wickless lantern and a stone basin. They've seen this craft before. Don't say it — let someone notice.

THE CAUSAL CHAIN (let them experiment — rolls only where marked):

1. The basin wants the sky. A star is carved in its dry bottom (Investigation DC 12 to spot, or FREE if anyone just pours water in to see). Water in the basin → the first evening star reflects → 📖 "The lantern kindles. Silver. No flame you've ever seen — but you've seen this light before."
2. The light IS the bridge. 📖 "The lantern's reflection lays a wandering silver line across the water — stepping stones just beneath the surface, visible only where its light falls." Someone carries the lantern and walks the line; the rest follow in the light.
3. The crossing: carrier DC 12 DEX (Acrobatics) at midstream (wind gust); each follower DC 10. Roped together = advantage (praise it out loud). Heavy armor = disadvantage — 📖 "the stones wobble under the weight."
4. If the lantern gutters (carrier fails by 5+, or dropped): 📖 "The path vanishes under your feet." Everyone still on the water: DC 12 STR (Athletics) or take 1d4 cold and drop one loose item to the current. 🗝 Rekindles if the carrier stands dead still for one full round while the basin still holds water. Tense, fixable.
5. Swim instead: allowed — DC 15 Athletics each, everything soaked (miserable camp, disadvantage on tonight's watch checks), and NO cache.
6. 🤫 The sprite REFUSES the lantern. Backs away, wings flat — same as the shrine. Crosses high and wide alone, waits subdued on the far bank. Say nothing.
7. Comedy valve: a coin dropped in the basin vanishes with a disappointed plip. 📖 "The basin is not a tip jar."

PAY IT FORWARD: hang the lantern on the far post + fill the far basin → 📖 "The flagstone beneath the post grinds aside. Below: an old ferryman's cache — oilcloth bundles, untouched for years." 🎲 THE FERRYMAN'S CACHE — EVERYONE ROLLS d100, one at a time, read each aloud. (Table: Appendix A.) 🗝 This is the session's big dice ritual — savor it.

Camp (short). 💬 one watch question: "What did you almost say at the ford — and didn't?" 🗝 The Tallyman's owed sleepless night: HOLD. Recommend collecting on the voyage, not tonight.
