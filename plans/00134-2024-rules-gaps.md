## Purpose
Building the 2014 rules (Plan 131) turned up five places where the default 2024 rules are wrong. Every 2024 table, including Justin's own, hits them:
- A long rest gives back half the Hit Dice instead of all of them.
- The phone hides "no action needed" options such as Action Surge.
- The level-up guide never asks for a class's later choices.
- The level-up guide never adds spells that become always prepared at later levels.
- Heavy armor's AC subtracts a negative DEX.

Justin asked for these fixed on 11/04. When done, 2024 follows its rules in all five places, and 2014 is unchanged.

## Context and Orientation
- **Long rest:** `services/rest_service.py`, `_hit_dice_regained` (Plan 131 F pulled it out). The 2014 PHB restores half your total Hit Dice (minimum 1); the 2024 PHB restores all spent Hit Dice.
- **Phone turn kit:** `frontend/src/pages/player/NowTab.tsx`, `const free = old ? … : …` (Plan 131 A gated it to 2014).
- **Heavy armor AC:** `services/character_builder_service.py` `_armor_class`, and the wizard mirror in `frontend/src/components/character-wizard/rules.ts`. Heavy armor: AC = the base, with no DEX at all (neither positive nor negative).
- **Level-up:** `services/levelup_service.py`.
  - The 2014 path builds `LevelUpPlan.class_choices` (`ChoiceStep`s whose count is the new picks: running total at the new level minus at the old) and applies them through `feat_rules.apply_options` / `apply_skills`.
  - It fills `SpellStep.always_prepared` from `rules.always_prepared(...)`.
  - The 2024 module (`levelup_2024`) has the same `CHOICE_KINDS`, `CLASS_CHOICES`, `SUBCLASS_CHOICES` and `class_choice_counts`, which the 2024 builder already uses.
  - The guide UI (`frontend/src/pages/LevelUpGuide.tsx`) already renders class choices for 2014.

## Outcomes and Retrospective
All five gaps were fixed in 659a639, with 1673 tests passing.
- 2024 PCs now get all spent Hit Dice back on a long rest.
- 2024 PCs see the "No action needed" panel on the phone.
- 2024 PCs are asked for their class's later choices and get always-prepared spells at level-up.

Saved sheets aren't recomputed. An existing heavy-armor PC with DEX below 10 keeps the AC it was saved with, and the DM fixes it on the sheet.

Lesson: when a second edition is added, check the first edition's tests for pinned bugs. Two of them here asserted the wrong 2024 behaviour.
