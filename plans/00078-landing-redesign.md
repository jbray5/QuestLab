## Purpose
Justin: "Can you make a fresh pass at our landing page for signing in or
signing up? The d20 doesn't look great. And do all of the boxes still
apply? Can we beautify and streamline?" The Plan 35 page led with a flat
hex-and-circle favicon blown up to 96 px, six feature boxes written before
the board, the cockpit, QR join, the character creator and shake-to-roll
existed, and the sign-in card below the fold.

## Shipped
- **`components/D20Mark.tsx`** — a true icosahedron: orthographic
  projection of the real solid, face 20 toward the viewer with a slight
  tilt, ten visible faces lit from the top-left on crimson resin, gold
  edges and numerals (20 centered; 2, 8, 14 adjacent; the outer ring
  numbered). Generated by `scratchpad/gen_d20.py`; `public/d20.svg` (the
  favicon) is regenerated from the same geometry.
- **`pages/Welcome.tsx`** rewritten. Top bar (wordmark, Guide, Terms, Sign
  in). Hero grid: the pitch on the left (die, eyebrow, "Run the table
  you've been picturing.", lede, three proof lines, the price line) and the
  account card on the right, above the fold at desktop widths. The card
  keeps every auth path: Discord / Patreon buttons, the Create account /
  Sign in segmented control with the password form, the personal-mode
  fallback, and the email-only card when a deployment has no accounts yet.
- Six boxes → **four pillars** that match the product as it is: the board
  on the TV, a living sheet on every phone, a DM cockpit, AI prep for
  patrons. Then **three numbered steps** (a real sequence): make a
  campaign, put the QR on the TV, stage a map and roll initiative.
- Footer adds "Not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast" beside the SRD
  notice. Phones: single column, the proof list yields to the pillars.
